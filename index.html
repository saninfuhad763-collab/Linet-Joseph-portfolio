Biography & Journey

Linet Joseph • Founder & Visionary Behind Eidalux

“From communicating stories in the media to creating a story of comfort through Eidalux.”

Linet Joseph is a Kerala entrepreneur whose journey reflects determination, adaptability, and a strong focus on quality and customer experience. Moving from media to manufacturing with ASACO and pioneering Eidalux, her visionary approach bridges communication, craftsmanship, and sleep health.

Foundation

From Media Sector to Entrepreneurship

Her professional career began in the media sector, including her association with Deepika Daily, where she developed valuable experience in communication, marketing, and understanding consumer needs. This experience later became an important foundation for her entrepreneurial journey.

Manufacturing

From Media to Mattress Manufacturing

Linet entered the mattress industry through ASACO Mattress, becoming deeply involved in manufacturing, marketing, and business development. Her vision went beyond simply producing mattresses—she focused on comfort, quality, support, and better sleep. Her customer-centric approach established the foundation for the next stage.

Brand Evolution

The Eidalux Journey

Eidalux Mattress represents the evolution of Linet Joseph's vision into a premium sleep brand. Eidalux focuses on craftsmanship, quality materials, technology, comfort, and better sleep, offering products designed for different comfort and support requirements. It reflects the evolution from a mattress manufacturing business towards a distinctive premium sleep and comfort brand.

Philosophy

Leadership & Entrepreneurial Philosophy

Linet's entrepreneurial philosophy is built around communication, courage, perseverance, innovation, and customer focus. Her journey demonstrates her willingness to embrace new challenges and build a business around customer needs. She also participates in entrepreneurship programmes, sharing the importance of creativity, perseverance, and risk-taking with students and young entrepreneurs.

Vision

Vision for Eidalux & Sleep Wellness

For Linet, a mattress is more than a household product—it is an investment in comfort, quality, and better sleep. Continuous evolution from early media experience to ASACO and Eidalux reflects a resolute vision to build a trusted premium sleep brand from Kerala.
