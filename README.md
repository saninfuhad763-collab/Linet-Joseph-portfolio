# Linet Joseph - Digital Profile & Portfolio

> Founder & Business Visionary Behind Eidalux Mattress | Mattress Industry Leader | Kerala Entrepreneur

## Overview
A responsive digital bio-sheet and portfolio web application for **Linet Joseph**, presenting her entrepreneurial journey from media to mattress manufacturing with ASACO, and pioneering the premium sleep brand **Eidalux Mattress**.

## Features
- **Biography & Entrepreneurial Journey**: Interactive milestone timeline detailing Linet Joseph's career, philosophy, and brand evolution.
- **Eidalux Mattress Collections**: Showcase of handcrafted luxury hybrid mattresses, orthopedic spine care systems, and 100% natural organic latex series.
- **Mattress Showcase Gallery**: High-resolution gallery of flagship Eidalux sleep systems.
- **Business Referral Slip**: Integrated member lead referral and slip submission via WhatsApp.
- **Direct Connect**: 1-click WhatsApp connect, direct phone call, email, vCard contact download, and profile QR code sharing.
- **Smooth Trackpad & Touch Scrolling**: Optimized modal dialogs with scroll protection and isolation.
- **Fully Responsive**: Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports with sticky quick-action bar.

## Tech Stack
- HTML5 / CSS3 (Custom Responsive Architecture)
- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- [Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/)
- [Lenis Smooth Scroll](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/)
- [GSAP & ScrollTrigger](https://greensock.com/gsap/)
